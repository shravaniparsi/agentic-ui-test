INTERPRETATION

Cohen's kappa between the two independent annotators is 0.000: observed
agreement (0.330) is exactly equal to agreement expected by chance (0.330).
The two annotators are not applying the taxonomy consistently with one another.

The result is not an artifact of one annotator. Each human also agrees with the
original single-annotator labels at chance (kappa +0.016 and -0.030), and the
three label distributions differ substantially, most visibly in the partial
category (42 original, 35 human1, 14 human2).

Taken together, three independent labellings of the same 100 failures agree at
chance. The obvious / deceptive / partial taxonomy, as operationalised here
(task text plus the agent's final screenshot, with the category definitions
shown on screen), is not reliably reproducible. This is a limitation of the
taxonomy and its labelling protocol, not evidence about the verifiers.

Any analysis that conditions on failure type should therefore be read as
descriptive of one annotator's labelling, and no claim in the paper should rest
on the distinction between deceptive and partial failures.
